HOW IT WORKS

Three Screens, Three Jobs

STAGE

The laptop on the projector

Owns the current slide. Hands out both links as codes on screen.

REMOTE

My phone, in my hand

Next and back, my notes, a timer, and a pointer. Nobody else can drive.

AUDIENCE

Everyone else's phones

The same slide, close up and readable, without squinting at the wall.

Nothing to install. Every role is the same web page, opened at a different link.

THE ONE STEP

Scan Once, Follow Everything

A four-character room code and a camera. From then on, every time I move, their screen moves.

1

I press L. Two codes appear on the projector.

2

They point a camera at the left one and tap the link.

3

That is it. No app, no sign-in, no typing a long URL.

The panel shows how many phones are in the room, live.
IN MY HAND

My Phone Is the Clicker

I never have to stand next to the laptop again. Everything a presenter view gives me, on the device already in my hand.

●

My notes, in full size. The ones already written into the slide.

●

What is coming next. So a transition never catches me out.

●

A running clock. Started the moment the deck went live.

●

Drag to point. A dot lands on the projected slide, where I aimed.

Only the phone that scanned my private code can move the deck. A room code alone does nothing.

YOUR CALL

How Much They Get to Wander

LOCKED

They watch, only

Swiping does nothing. Use it when the order of the argument matters.

FOLLOW · DEFAULT

They follow, but may stray

Wander back a slide to re-read, then tap one button to catch up.

OPEN

The whole deck, theirs

For a workshop, or the moment you stop talking and they start reading.

Switch between them mid-sentence. There is also a quiet "I'm lost" tap that only I see.

THE BORING PART THAT MATTERS

Nothing Loads Mid-Sentence

Before the deck will call itself live, it goes through every slide behind a cover: fonts resolved, images decoded, each slide actually drawn once, the screen locked awake, the connection joined.

Fonts ready Images decoded Every slide drawn Screen kept awake Room joined

And if the wifi gives out, the sync quietly stops and the deck keeps working like any other deck. It can never take the presentation down with it.
